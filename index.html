Our Story

The Luxury

Relax & Reconnect

Every stay here offers a thoughtful escape designed to slow down, breathe deeply, reconnect, and feel renewed.

Nature-inspired spaces, elegant cottages, peaceful gardens, and open gathering areas — arranged around moments of calm and connection. Each retreat balances contemporary comfort with natural beauty. Private rooms create a quiet atmosphere, while generous outdoor spaces invite guests to pause, explore, and simply unwind.

Every detail is thoughtfully crafted with warm materials, refined textures, and sustainable touches that make each space feel effortless. Around them: beautifully designed rooms, nourishing cuisine, immersive experiences, scenic trails, refreshing pools, and welcoming spaces created for meaningful moments together.

Stay Here

The Art of Slowing Down

At Sambodhi Retreat, time feels a little different. Surrounded by nature and the peaceful spirit of Bodh Gaya, every moment invites you to pause, breathe, and simply be.

Wake up to gentle mornings, unwind by the pool, stroll through the green surroundings, or enjoy quiet moments in the comfort of your room. Here, there is no need to rush. From tranquil spaces and warm hospitality to thoughtful experiences inspired by the essence of Bodh Gaya, Sambodhi is a place where your day unfolds at its own pace.
